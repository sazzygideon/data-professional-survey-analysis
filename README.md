# 📊 Data Professional Survey Analysis

### 📄 Overview
This project analyzes survey data from over 600 data professionals to understand trends in **salary, job satisfaction, and work-life balance** across different industries and countries.

![Dashboard Preview](https://github.com/sazzygideon/data-professional-survey-analysis/blob/4a5759e7551dd72eb33d36fb36dc05fb4df2d833/dashboard_screenshot.PNG)

### ❓ Business Problem
The goal was to answer key questions for aspiring data professionals:
* What is the average salary by job title and experience?
* Which programming languages are most popular (Python vs. R)?
* How does job satisfaction rate vary by country?

### 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Data Cleaning:** Excel (Power Query)
* **Visualization:** Power BI
* **Data Source:** Survey Data via [Alex The Analyst]

### 🔎 Key Insights & Findings
* **Salary Trends:** Data Scientists consistently reported higher average salaries compared to Data Analysts, with a significant jump in the "100k-125k" bracket.
* **Language:** Python is the clear favorite, chosen by over **70%** of respondents as their primary language.
* **Satisfaction:** Work-Life Balance rated higher on average (6.8/10) than Salary Satisfaction (5.4/10) across all roles.
* **Entry Barrier:** Over **50%** of respondents rated breaking into the industry as "Difficult," identifying "Building a Portfolio" as the best strategy.

### 🧹 Data Transformation
The raw data required cleaning in Power Query before analysis:
1.  **Split Columns:** Separated 'Country' to isolate specific locations.
2.  **Standardized Values:** Grouped salary ranges for consistent sorting.
3.  **Removed Duplicates:** Ensured unique responses based on ID.
